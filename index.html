HOW IT WORKS

The signal never asks permission.

Most prosthetics wait for you to flex a spare muscle or tap a phone app. Axiom's interface sits directly against the nerve bundle left at the residual limb and listens for the same electrical pattern that used to reach your hand.

A small classifier trained on your own signal — not a generic model — learns the difference between "reach" and "grip" over your first two weeks, then gets out of the way.

0ms

The limb reports back, too. Pressure sensors in the fingertips route a compressed signal to the remaining nerve, so gripping a paper cup and gripping a wrench feel distinct again — not just visually, but in the hand.

Everything runs locally on the limb's own processor. No signal leaves your body to be trained, sold, or lost in an outage.
